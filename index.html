ABOUT THIS PROJECT — A LIVING WORK

The words
are ours.
The questions
are open.

The Human Words We Give Machines is a developing dictionary about the metaphors, borrowed concepts, and human attributes that populate the language of artificial intelligence.

It is neither an argument that machines are human nor a blanket rejection of analogy. It asks for clearer distinctions, stronger evidence, and more care in what our words imply.
